Healthy Technology Habits

10 Steps to a Healthy Digital Lifestyle

  1. Set reasonable limits for your daily screen time
  2. Take a 5–10 minute break every hour when using devices
  3. Put devices away at least 30 minutes before sleeping
  4. Get 8–10 hours of sleep every night
  5. Stay physically active — exercise or play outdoors regularly
  6. Spend quality time with family and friends without screens
  7. Protect your eyes — maintain proper distance and good lighting
  8. Be kind and respectful to everyone online
  9. Think before you click, share, or post
  10. Balance your digital life with real-world experiences

Quick Reminders

  • 💙 Be kind: Treat others online the same way you would in person
  • 🔒 Stay safe: Never share your password or personal information
  • ⚖️ Balance is key: Real-life experiences are just as important as digital ones!
  • ⏰ Time wisely: Use technology to help you, not to distract you
