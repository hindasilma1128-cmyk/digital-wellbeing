Using Technology Responsibly

Manage Your Screen Time

Set reasonable limits for screen use and take regular breaks. Make sure that using phones, computers, or other devices does not interfere with sleep, schoolwork, or important responsibilities.

Source: share.google

Practice Good Digital Habits

  • Be respectful when communicating online — treat others the way you want to be treated
  • Protect personal information — never share passwords, addresses, or private details
  • Think carefully before posting or sharing — once online, it stays online
  • Check whether information is reliable before spreading it to others
  • Ask a trusted adult if you encounter something uncomfortable or confusing online

Balance Online and Offline Activities

Source: share.google

Technology should not replace physical activities, hobbies, family time, and friendships. Creating a healthy balance between online and offline activities helps support overall well-being and mental health.
