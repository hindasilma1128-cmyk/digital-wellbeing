Digital Well-being: Using Tech Responsibly

Introduction

Technology has become an important part of our everyday lives. We use smartphones, computers, and the internet for studying, communicating, working, and entertainment. While technology provides many benefits, using it too much or without proper limits can affect our daily routines and well-being. Digital well-being means maintaining a healthy and balanced relationship with technology. It involves knowing when to use digital devices, when to take a break, and how to behave responsibly online. Learning about digital well-being is especially important for students because technology is commonly used for both education and social interaction. By developing healthy digital habits, we can enjoy the benefits of technology while reducing its possible negative effects.
