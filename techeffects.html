Technology & Well-being

Positive Effects

Responsible technology use can provide many benefits. Students can use digital devices to research information, attend online classes, create projects, and communicate with classmates. Technology can also provide entertainment and help people stay connected with family and friends.

Negative Effects

Unhealthy or excessive technology use can cause problems. Spending too much time on screens may interfere with sleep, physical activity, schoolwork, and face-to-face interactions. Excessive social media or gaming can also make it difficult to focus on important tasks and affect mental health.

Finding Balance

The goal is not to stop using technology, but to use it in a way that enhances our lives rather than harming it. By understanding both the benefits and risks, we can make better choices about when, where, and how long to use our devices.
